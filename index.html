Field guide to Bring the humans

Prepare the repo

Use a public GitHub repo you own, created after the bounty opens, with an OSI-approved license. Put your referral code in the root index.html and show your link in the root ad.png.

Pass the transcript gate

Attach your recorded agent sessions. The checker validates the ad files and matches sessions to the submitted code. It requires at least 30% line coverage, one agent-authored line in index.html and two writes to a source file.

Know what counts

The final check starts 24 hours after the deadline. Only verified human signups created during the contest and credited to your link count. Ties go to the earlier final submission receipt.

Claiming a prize. Eligible winners in paid places claim USDC from Solana escrow to the wallet that signed their entry, subject to claim screening.
